The premise

The Cartesian error

For four hundred years, modern science has assumed that consciousness is an emergent property of computational complexity. When technology is designed by people who believe consciousness arises from matter, it tends to treat humans as machines to be optimised.

RAI begins from the opposite premise. Consciousness is primary. Matter unfolds from it. That single shift changes what we build, and why.

The mark & construction

A considered geometry, built on the grid

  • Angular, built from straight cuts, suggesting precision and intelligence.
  • The open “R” reads as a path, or a spark of insight.
  • Set in one weight, balanced and quiet, never loud.
  • Key lines fall on the golden divisions, 0.382 and 0.618.
  • Clear space equals the width of the R on every side.
  • Minimum size 96px wide on screen, 22mm in print.
